# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Robot/Shift welcomes respectful, specific, evidence-led collaboration.
Participants must:

- be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- critique ideas and implementation, not people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, and
  deliberate disruption;
- protect private data and never pressure someone to disclose credentials or
  personal information;
- respect copyright, attribution, and compatible licensing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation.

For a private conduct report, open a
[private GitHub advisory](https://github.com/rjchakraborty/robot-shift/security/advisories/new)
with `Conduct report` in the title. Include links and a factual description;
remove secrets and unnecessary personal data. Reports will be reviewed
privately, consistently, and with the minimum disclosure needed to act.
